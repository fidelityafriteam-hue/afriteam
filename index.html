About TeamAfriSale

TeamAfriSale is the market brand of Fidelity Africa Team Ltd, focused on sales, marketing, sourcing, supply, importation and delivery coordination.

Our starting point is Kenya's coastal region, including Diani, Ukunda and Mombasa, with ambitions to serve more customers across Kenya and African markets.

Our approach: Let others flow — making it easier for customers and suppliers to connect.

We aim to make sourcing straightforward: share your requirements, review the quotation and agreed terms, then confirm whether to proceed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you source a product that is not listed?

Yes, you can send a sourcing request. We will check whether a suitable supplier can be found and confirm availability before you commit.

Do you deliver outside Diani and Mombasa?

Tell us your town or delivery location. Delivery coverage, cost and timing must be confirmed for each order.

How do I know the final price?

Ask for a quotation. Confirm the product specifications, quantity, total cost, delivery charges, lead time and payment terms before paying.

Can I pay online through M-Pesa on the website?

Website M-Pesa checkout is not yet confirmed as active. Please use only payment instructions provided and verified directly by TeamAfriSale.

Does sending a request confirm my order?

No. A request is an enquiry only. Your order is confirmed after availability, quotation, delivery arrangements and payment terms are agreed.
